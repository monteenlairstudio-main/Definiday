2026-11-06 owner chose the visual style « Correspondance + rigueur Édition » (design/styles/correspondance/style.md and board; Édition for typographic discipline: design/styles/edition/style.md). Daily word = a postcard/letter from Glose: stamp in the word's colours with its initial, dated postmark, Seyès notebook for the example, evening quiz as a letter opening « Bonsoir, », collection as a stamp album (« PORTÉ » cancel), one yarn thread tying elements. Rigueur Édition: ONE serif for the interface (Libre Baskerville), Courier Prime for postal labels, Dancing Script only for Glose's notes (never sole carrier of info), small caps, clean rules, at most ONE strong decorative element per screen. Per-word palettes kept. Fixes from docs/audits/revue_ui_011.md go in with the restyle. The other two directions (Édition, Herbier) stay in design/styles/ as references.
